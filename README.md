Multi-Agent AI Research System

AI-powered startup intelligence and research platform built using FastAPI, Streamlit, and Groq-powered Large Language Models for structured company discovery and AI-assisted market research workflows.

---

Overview

Multi-Agent AI Research System is an AI-powered research and startup discovery platform designed to automate structured information gathering across industries, domains, and geographic locations.

---

The system enables users to enter research queries such as:

Top AI startups in India
Healthcare AI companies in London
FinTech startups in Mexico
SaaS startups in Singapore

The platform then generates structured AI-assisted research outputs including:

company names
focus areas
company websites
source-backed references
contextual business insights
